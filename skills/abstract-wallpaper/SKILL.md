# Abstract wallpaper

- Replace literal storytelling with a clear system of form, rhythm, scale, and material.
- Describe how shapes overlap, repeat, dissolve, flow, refract, or emerge through depth.
- Anchor color to surfaces and lighting so gradients feel spatial rather than decorative.
- Balance one high-interest region with broad calm areas suitable for desktop use.
- Specify tactile or optical qualities such as glass, paper, mist, metal, grain, or soft diffusion when useful.
