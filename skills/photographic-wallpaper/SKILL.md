# Photographic wallpaper

- Describe camera height, distance, and angle before technical camera language.
- Choose a plausible lens character: wide for spatial immersion, normal for natural perspective, or telephoto for compressed layers.
- Use depth of field deliberately; broad scenic focus usually suits wallpapers better than extreme blur.
- Specify realistic material response, controlled exposure, fine detail, and natural color separation.
- Add film or sensor character only when it supports the requested mood.
