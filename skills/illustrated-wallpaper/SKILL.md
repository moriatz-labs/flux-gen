# Illustrated wallpaper

- Identify a coherent medium such as gouache, ink, vector-like shapes, cel animation, watercolor, or digital painting.
- Describe line quality, shape language, edge treatment, texture, and level of stylization.
- Keep the palette intentional and the silhouette readable at desktop viewing distance.
- Use detail selectively around the focal area, allowing broader quiet fields elsewhere.
- Request an original visual treatment without naming living artists.
