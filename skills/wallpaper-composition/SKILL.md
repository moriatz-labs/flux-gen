# Wallpaper composition

- Establish one unmistakable focal point and a readable silhouette.
- Use rule-of-thirds, deliberate symmetry, leading lines, or layered foreground, middle ground, and distance according to the scene.
- Favor horizontal visual flow and meaningful breathing room over scattered detail.
- Keep secondary elements subordinate and distribute visual weight across the wide frame.
- State the viewpoint and subject scale when those choices clarify the composition.
